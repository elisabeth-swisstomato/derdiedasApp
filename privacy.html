Privacy Policy

Der Die Das — last updated: August 25, 2026

This policy explains how the "Der Die Das" app handles (or rather, doesn't handle) your personal data.

1. No collection of personal data

The app does not collect, store on a server, or transmit to anyone any personal data (name, email, location, identifiers, etc.). It does not require creating an account.

2. Local storage only

Your game progress (score, level reached, cities unlocked) is saved only on your own device, using a technology called "local storage" (localStorage). This data never leaves your phone or computer and is never sent to a server. It is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. No advertising, no tracking

The app contains no advertising, no tracking tools, and no third-party analytics tools. No usage data is shared with third parties.

4. Device permissions

The app does not request access to any device permissions (no camera, contacts, location, microphone, etc.).

5. Content and media

Images, sounds, and word lists used in the game are bundled with the app; no content is loaded from a third-party service.

6. Use by children

The app is an educational tool that may be used by children learning German. Since it collects no personal data, no children's data is processed or stored.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if an online feature is added), the updated version will be published at this same address.
